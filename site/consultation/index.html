The software runs about $10k to implement and $500 to $2,000 a month after that. At your stage that money is better spent finding the first few deals. So here is the free stack I actually used to do exactly that.

  • RegridParcel boundaries, owner names, acreage.
  • Google Earth ProFree. Measures distances and shows historical imagery.
  • County property appraiser sitesOwnership, values, sale history, building size. The single best free source there is.
  • Clerk of courtLis pendens, evictions, probate. Distress months before anyone else sees it.
  • SunbizTurns a Florida LLC into a real person’s name.
  • TruePeopleSearchTurns that name into a phone number.
  • TerrakottaCold calling, so you can actually work the list you just built.
  • LoopNet and Crexi free tiersAsking rates. Enough to build your own comp set by hand.

That stack costs nothing and it is genuinely how this started. Build the list, then start calling.
