# Hyde Park Village → industrial filming trip — property shortlist

**Purpose:** scout on-market dock-high industrial buildings close enough to Hyde Park Village
to shoot in one day: Hyde Park Village content → Tesla drive B-roll → industrial property content.

**Anchor:** Hyde Park Village, 742 S Village Cir, Tampa, FL 33606.
**Verified:** 2026-08-23. Distances/drive times are approximate (Google-map estimates, midday, no traffic).
**All listings are third-party — none are ours. Access requires a call to the listing broker.**

---

## The drive (this is the B-roll)

Hyde Park Village → **Lee Roy Selmon Expressway east** → exit at 22nd St / Adamo Dr.
Elevated expressway the whole way, downtown Tampa skyline out the driver's-side window,
then a hard visual cut from Bayshore-money to heavy-industrial Adamo. That contrast is the shot.
Every property below is off the same drive, so one route covers all of them.

## Logistics to settle before the shoot day

1. **Access.** All six are other brokers' listings. Film access = a call to the listing
   agent, not a lockbox. Vacant suites are the easy yes; occupied buildings are a no.
   The C&W ones (#4, #6) and Colliers (#5) are agency-listed and the most likely to
   just hand over a key for an hour.
2. **Verify two.** #3 (4020 E Adamo) may be off market. #2 (1601 N 39th) has a listing
   dated Nov 2023. Call both before building a schedule around them.
3. **Rates.** #1 and #5 have published rates ($12.50 and $12.75/SF). The rest are
   "upon request" — ask on the access call if we want them on camera.
4. **Book two, shoot one.** Get permission at #1 and #4. They are 15 minutes apart on the
   same route, and one gives the gritty look while the other gives the clean look.
